Overcoming the Inaccessibility of Public Transport for Senior Citizens and Disabled People.

01
FIRST STOP • UNDERSTANDING THE CHALLENGE

The Social Problem

Core Issue: Persons with disabilities, senior citizens, and commuters with reduced mobility face systemic exclusion from civic, social, and economic opportunities in urban India due to fragmented and inaccessible last-mile transportation infrastructure.

♿

Root Causes

While cities build modern transit hubs, the surrounding infrastructure—broken footpaths, missing tactile paving, high-floor feeder autos, and top-down planning without user input—creates an impassable barrier.

♧

Consequences

This "last-mile gap" strips citizens of personal independence, forces reliance on family or costly private cabs, and restricts their fundamental right to freedom of movement.

◈

Relevance & Significance

In rapidly urbanizing India, inaccessible public transit actively blocks millions of marginalized citizens from reaching schools, workplaces, and hospitals, turning an infrastructure gap into lifelong socio-economic inequality.

02
SECOND STOP • OUR VISION

Policy Objectives

SAKSHAM aims to transform public transportation into a space where accessibility is a standard, not a privilege.

✧

Universal Access

Make railway stations and trains accessible to passengers with diverse mobility needs.

♧

Passenger Safety

Reduce travel-related risks through safer infrastructure and assistance.

◇

Independence

Enable passengers to travel with greater confidence and dignity.

◎

Equal Opportunity

Ensure mobility limitations do not restrict access to public transport.

✦

Inclusive Services

Make passenger information and support available to all.

♜

Accountability

Establish regular accessibility audits and feedback systems.

03
THIRD STOP • THE PROPOSED SOLUTION

Proposed Measures

A combination of infrastructure, technology and human support will help make every journey more accessible.

♿

Accessible Infrastructure

Ramps, lifts, tactile pathways, accessible toilets and safer platform access.

▣

Reserved Spaces

Clearly marked seating and designated wheelchair spaces in trains.

◉

Digital Assistance

A simple booking and assistance system for passengers requiring support.

♬

Inclusive Information

Audio announcements, visual displays and clear multilingual signage.

✚

Trained Personnel

Dedicated staff to assist passengers at stations and during journeys.

◈

Emergency Support

Accessible help points and priority response for passengers needing assistance.

04
FOURTH STOP • PUTTING THE POLICY INTO ACTION

Implementation Strategy

SAKSHAM will be implemented through coordinated action between railway authorities, station administrations and accessibility experts.

01 Accessibility audits of stations and trains
02 Phased infrastructure improvements
03 Recruitment and training of support staff
04 Introduction of digital assistance facilities
05 Regular safety inspections and maintenance
06 Passenger feedback and grievance redressal

Practicality and Implementation

The policy can be implemented gradually by government and transport authorities by upgrading existing buses, stations and bus stops with ramps, lifts, handrails and priority seating. Staff training and regular accessibility inspections can also be introduced. Although some changes may require additional funding, implementing them in phases will make the policy practical and affordable.

Monitoring: Progress will be reviewed through accessibility audits, passenger feedback, service inspections and periodic performance reports.

05
FIFTH STOP • WHY THIS POLICY MATTERS

Policy Justification

Public transportation is an essential public service. Its benefits must be available to every citizen, regardless of age or physical ability.

⚖

Right to Equality

Supports equal access and non-discrimination in public services.

♧

Human Dignity

Reduces unnecessary dependence and promotes independent mobility.

◎

Social Inclusion

Encourages participation in education, employment and everyday life.

The policy reflects the principles of accessibility and inclusion recognised under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 and the broader vision of universal design.
